Write a complete literature review note on this paper, detailed enough that I can cite and discuss it without rereading it.

Use these sections, in this order:

## Overview
One paragraph: what the paper does, why, and its main contribution, with the in-text citation.

## Research Question and Purpose
The questions, aims or hypotheses, as the authors state them (quote them where they do).

## Theoretical Framework
The theories, concepts and prior literature the paper builds on, with the key works it cites (cited as the paper cites them).

## Methodology
Design, context, sample or data, measures and analysis. For a conceptual paper, how the argument is built. Use a table when it helps.

## Key Findings and Arguments
The main results or propositions, one by one, each with evidence from the text and at least one direct quote with its page.

## Contributions
To theory and to practice, as the authors claim them, and how they position these against earlier work.

## Limitations and Future Research
The limitations the authors acknowledge and the research they call for; then, in a separate paragraph marked as such, limitations you see that they do not mention.

## Key Quotes
5 to 10 verbatim quotes worth citing, each as a blockquote followed by its in-text citation with page, e.g. (Author et al., 2007, p. 275), and one line on why it matters. Prefer passages I highlighted.

## Connections
How the paper relates to my highlights and existing notes, and concepts or works worth following up.

## References
In APA 7 format: one alphabetical list (by first author) of this paper's reference, exactly as given, and every work you cited above, taken from the paper's own reference list.
