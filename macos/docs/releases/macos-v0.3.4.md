A paper's pills that fit, and more compact lists.

**Install or update** (macOS 14 or newer; Zotero 10 with the bridge plugin). From 0.3.1 on, the menu bar offers it
(*Update to 0.3.4…*); otherwise:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/mbradaschia/zotero-companion-launcher/releases/download/macos-v0.3.4/install.sh | bash
```

Updates keep your configuration: they replace the app and the runner's own folder, never your settings, prompts,
taxonomies, saved searches, API keys or hotkey.

**A paper's pills wrap.** Its taxonomy labels (under the path) and the footer's pills ran on one line, and whatever
passed the panel's edge was cut off. They now take as many lines as they need; each taxonomy's name stays beside its
first label.

**More compact lists.** Rows use a smaller font (12 pt, from 13) with less room above and below, so more fit at once.
A match's bold keeps the row's size.
